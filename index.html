The Band

Where splendor meets the street.

Fashion of Splendor is a premium foreday morning band in the Barbados Crop Over festival — the pre-dawn street party where revellers hit the road in paint, powder and costume behind booming music trucks until sunrise.

We built our name on one thing: Champagne Soca — taking the energy of foreday and giving it a premium finish. Fully-stocked bars, bold sections, the island's hottest DJs, and a jump that's talked about long after the sun's up.

Last season we brought The Drama. For Foreday 2027, we're going bigger.
